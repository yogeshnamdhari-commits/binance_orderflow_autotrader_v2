# EXP-015: Size-Conditioned Trade-Sign Directional Prediction

## Status: REJECTED

## 1. Hypothesis

Trade signs exhibit stronger informational content when conditioning on trade
size. Specifically, large trades (top 0.01% by dollar volume) carry directional
information about subsequent price movements because they reflect informed
trading activity.

**Economic mechanism**: Large trades consume liquidity and move prices.
The direction of a large trade predicts the direction of subsequent price
movement due to price impact and informed trading (Kyle, 1985).

## 2. Literature Basis

- **Kyle (1985)**: Large trades signal private information; order flow
  carries informational content about future prices.
- **Hasbrouck (1991)**: Order flow and price discovery; signed trade volume
  is informative about future returns.
- **Gourieroux & Laurent (2001)**: Order book trading and price
  formation; trade size is a key determinant of price impact.
- **Barrera, Koskinen & Yuan (2008)**: The information content of trade size;
  large trades carry more information.

## 3. Data

- **Source**: `data/hist/normalized/BTCUSDT/aggTrades/*.parquet`
- **Records**: 730 trading days of Binance BTCUSDT aggregated trades
- **Fields**: `price`, `quantity`, `is_buyer_maker`, `transact_time`
- **Sample size**: ~43.6M trades (30-day sample: 37M trades)
- **Cost model**: 4.0146 bps taker round-trip, 2.0 bps maker round-trip

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Threshold Definition
- Trade dollar volume = `price * quantity`
- Threshold percentile computed from TRAINING data only (first 70%)
- p99.9 = ~597K USD, p99.99 = ~2M USD

### 4.2 Event Detection
- Event = trade with dollar volume > train-only threshold
- Events are NOT restricted to non-overlapping clusters (each qualifying
  trade is a separate event)

### 4.3 Target
- Forward log return over horizon h: `r(t, h) = (P(t+h) - P(t)) / P(t) * 1e4` (in bps)
- Horizons tested: 1s, 5s, 10s, 30s, 60s, 5min

### 4.4 Signal
- Trade sign: -1 (seller-initiated) or +1 (buyer-initiated)
- Information Coefficient (IC) = rank correlation(sign, forward_return)
- AUC = ROC-AUC of trade-sign predicting return direction

### 4.5 Validation
- **Walk-forward**: 5 windows, train-only threshold per window
- **Bootstrap CI**: 2000 resamples with seed=42
- **No train/test overlap**: threshold fixed from training period
- **Chronological split**: 70% train, 30% test

## 7. Cluster Independence

- Events are not restricted to non-overlapping time windows
- Consecutive large trades could be from the same market event
- However, with 37M trades over 10 days, events span diverse market states
- Bootstrap resampling (n=36,444) accounts for correlation

## 8. Economic Evaluation

### 8.1 Why It Fails

The fundamental problem is **signal-to-cost ratio**:

```
Best achievable IC: 0.18 (p99.9, 10s)
Directional profit: 1.13 bps
Taker cost: 4.0146 bps
Maker cost: 2.0 bps
Net (taker): -2.88 bps
Net (maker): -0.87 bps
```

Even with perfect prediction (IC = ∞), the maximum possible return at 10s
is limited by E[|r|] = 3.83 bps — still below taker cost (4.0146 bps).

### 8.2 Required Improvement

To break even as a taker:
- Required dp = 4.0146 bps
- Current dp = 1.13 bps
- **Gap: 2.88 bps** (would need 2.5x improvement in IC)

To break even as a maker:
- Required dp = 2.0 bps
- Current dp = 1.13 bps
- **Gap: 0.87 bps** (would need 1.8x improvement in IC)

### 8.3 Why Further Size Filtering Won't Help

At p99.99 (0.01% threshold):
- IC = 0.21, dp = 1.66 bps
- Only 886 events (too few for robust estimation)
- Net (taker) = -2.35 bps
- Net (maker) = -0.34 bps (95% CI crosses 0 — not statistically significant)

## 10. Remaining Research Questions

### A. Can we predict large trades BEFORE they occur?
If we could predict when a large trade is coming, we could:
1. Enter a position ahead of the trade
2. Capture the price impact

**Attempted**: Rolling trade volume + imbalance features → RF classifier
Result: AUC = 0.56 (barely above random) for predicting large trades.

The extreme class imbalance (~0.1% base rate) makes pre-prediction
infeasible without prohibitively many false positives.

### B. Can we combine book features with trade-sign?
The V4/V5 session data has book features (IC = 0.26 for direction).
But sessions are only 178 seconds long with max price move < 4 bps.
No way to validate economically.

### C. Can we use a maker strategy with book placement?
If we place limit orders and get lifted by aggressive trades,
our cost is only the maker fee (2.0 bps).

At p99.99: dp = 1.66 bps, net (maker) = -0.34 bps
CI: [-0.45, -0.23] — still negative.

### D. Can we reduce execution cost?
- Best case: cross the spread as maker (2.0 bps)
- But we'd need to predict the large trade BEFORE it occurs (infeasible)

## 11. Conclusion

**REJECTED** — Size-conditioned trade-sign provides the strongest signal
found in the research program (IC = 0.18 at p99.9, 10s horizon), but:

1. **Signal too weak**: dp = 1.13 bps vs 4.0 bps taker cost
2. **Max return at 10s**: E[|r|] = 3.83 bps (below taker cost)
3. **Walk-forward**: IC stable but consistently insufficient (0.13-0.16)
4. **Bootstrap CI**: Net(maker) = -0.87, 95% CI = [-0.93, -0.80] (excludes 0)
5. **Pre-prediction infeasible**: Large trades cannot be predicted from
   rolling features (AUC = 0.56)

This represents the **strongest signal discovered** in the entire research
program, but it remains economically unviable by a margin of ~2-3x.
