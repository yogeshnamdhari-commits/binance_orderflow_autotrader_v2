# EXP-008: Volatility-Regime Conditional Trading

**Hypothesis**: The directional signal from order-flow features is stronger in HIGH volatility regimes, where the signal-to-cost ratio improves because moves are larger.

**Economic mechanism**:
- V5-V7 found gross +0.02 to +0.05 bps in ALL regimes
- But in high-vol regimes, typical moves are larger (std 2-3× low-vol)
- If the directional signal (P(correct)) is constant but move size scales with vol,
  then net expectancy could be positive in high-vol regimes alone

**Difference from V5-V8**:
- V5-V8: Single model, trade in ALL regimes
- EXP-008: Regime-conditional, only trade when vol > threshold

**Falsification criterion**:
- If net expectancy is not positive in ANY regime, reject
- If signal (dir accuracy) does not improve in high-vol, reject
