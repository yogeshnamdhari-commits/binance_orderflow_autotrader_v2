# EXP-012: Aggressive Flow × Absorption Capacity × Liquidity Fragility

**Status**: PROPOSED

**Research Priority**: Highest

## 1. Research Basis

### 1.1 Primary Literature

**Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (2013/2014)** — "The Price Impact of Order Book Events"
- Journal of Financial Econometrics, Vol. 12(1), pp. 47-88
- **Key finding**: Price changes are driven by order flow imbalance (OFI), with a linear relation `∆P = β × OFI` where `β = c/D` (impact coefficient inversely proportional to market depth)
- **Implication**: When aggressive flow is large relative to available depth, price impact is amplified. The conditional ratio `aggressive_flow / absorption_capacity` is the key state variable.

**Gould & Bonart (2016)** — "Queue Imbalance as a One-Tick-Ahead Price Predictor"
- Quantitative Finance, Vol. 16
- **Key finding**: Queue imbalance (bid/ask depth ratio) predicts direction of next mid-price movement. Local logistic regression reveals two regimes: steep response near equilibrium (|I| < 0.25), flat response in extreme imbalance states.
- **Implication**: Queue imbalance has conditional predictive power — strongest near equilibrium, not in extreme states.

**Explainable Patterns in Cryptocurrency Microstructure (2025)** — arXiv:2602.00776
- Binance Futures BTC/ETH perps, 2022-2025
- **Key finding**: Order flow imbalance, spread, and VWAP-to-mid deviations dominate SHAP importance across all crypto assets. Wider spreads associate with diminished predictability (adverse selection).
- **Implication**: The signal is conditional on liquidity state — wider spread = less predictable.

### 1.2 Crypto-Specific Liquidity Research

**Crypto Liquidity Analysis: Read Order Books (Kalena, 2026)**
- **Key findings**:
  - Top 10 BTC perpetual levels turn over entirely within seconds during active sessions
  - Order book half-life: seconds to low single-digit minutes during active hours
  - Depth regeneration speed is critical: fast = resilient, slow = breakout risk
  - High cancellation rates indicate spoofing or market-maker repositioning
  - "Absorption without price decline" is one of the strongest signals in crypto liquidity analysis

**Flash Crash Analysis (CoinMetrics, 2025)**
- During stress events: order book depth collapses, slippage increases non-linearly
- Larger trades disproportionately impact volatility (liquidity fragmentation)
- Market makers remove liquidity during volatility spikes (adverse selection pricing-in)

### 1.3 Synthesis

The research converges on a conditional mechanism:
1. **Aggressive flow** (market orders) creates demand for liquidity at the touch
2. **Absorption capacity** (available depth at best bid/ask) determines how much flow is absorbed without price movement
3. **Liquidity fragility** (cancellation velocity, depth regeneration, spread expansion) determines whether remaining liquidity is stable or vulnerable

When flow >> capacity AND fragility is high, price impact is large and directional. This is the conditional state where predictability exceeds execution costs.

## 2. Hypothesis

### Research Question
> When does aggressive order flow generate executable price movement exceeding transaction costs?

### Economic Mechanism
The price impact model `∆P = β × OFI` from Cont-Kukanov-Stoikov has slope `β = c/D`. When:
- `aggressive_flow` (market order size hitting the book) is large relative to `absorption_capacity` (depth at best bid/ask)
- AND the book is **fragile** (high cancellation rate, slow regeneration, wide spread)

Then `β` increases (depth is thin/volatile), amplifying price impact. The resulting move can exceed the 2.0-2.5 bps execution cost.

In stable, deep markets: aggressive flow is absorbed, `β` is small, moves are tiny (< 2.0 bps), no edge.

### Difference from All Previous Experiments
| Previous Experiments | EXP-012 |
|---|---|
| OFI → BUY/SELL model | Conditional: Flow/Capacity × Fragility → State → Model |
| Trade in all regimes | Only trade when state = high impact |
| Net expectancy: -1.9 to -2.6 bps | Hypothesis: +X bps conditional on state |
| Raw predictive features | Conditional state features + economic gate |

### Formal Hypotheses
- **H0 (Null)**: There is no market state where aggressive flow × absorption capacity × liquidity fragility produces net positive expectancy after execution costs.
- **H1 (Alternative)**: There exists a conditional market state where the ratio of aggressive flow to absorption capacity, combined with liquidity fragility indicators, produces economically significant price moves that exceed execution costs.

### Falsification Criteria
1. If net expectancy < 0 at ALL conditional states → REJECT
2. If 95% CI of net expectancy includes 0 → REJECT
3. If signal exists only in <5% of events (no capacity) → REJECT
4. If signal disappears after purging → REJECT
5. If feature leakage is detected → REJECT

  - `top_of_book_turnover` — rate of best bid/ask changes
  - `depth_persistence` — autocorrelation of depth at top levels

## 4. Target Design

### Horizons
Horizon selection based on data dynamics:
- **1s**: Matches Binance update frequency, captures immediate impact
- **3s**: Captures one full order book cycle (arrival + replenishment)
- **5s**: Standard microstructure horizon
- **10s**: Captures regime evolution

These are predeclared — NOT chosen based on OOS results.

### Labels
For each event at time t, for each horizon h:
- `r_h` = forward return in bps at horizon h (mid-price change)
- `direction_h` = sign(r_h)
- `executed_price_h` = first trade price at or after t+h (for execution cost modeling)

## 5. Expected Mechanism

1. **High flow-to-depth ratio**: Large market orders consume multiple price levels → price impact
2. **High cancellation velocity**: Liquidity is being withdrawn → remaining depth is unreliable → price continues moving
3. **Slow depth regeneration**: Consumed liquidity isn't replenished → no absorption for next wave of flow
4. **Wide/split spread**: Indicates adverse selection tension → market makers charging premium

When ALL conditions align:
- Aggressive flow overwhelms available depth
- Remaining liquidity is fragile (will vanish)
- Price moves persistently and large enough to cover costs

When conditions DON'T align:
- Flow is absorbed (ratio low) → no move
- Liquidity is stable (low fragility) → price reverts
- Spread is tight → no adverse selection → no sustained move

## 6. Cost Model

Round-trip execution cost:
- Maker fee: 0.02 bps (0.01% × 0.2)
- Taker fee: 0.04 bps (0.02% × 0.2)
- Spread cost: spread_bps (crossing the spread)
- Slippage: modeled as function of flow/depth ratio
- Latency allowance: 1ms per event (conservative)
- Adverse selection: estimated from signed_vol_500

Total cost gate: must exceed 2.0 bps net to be deployable.

## 7. Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Feature Engineering (`app/exp012_features.py`)
- Load v4 derived events with level snapshots
- Compute aggressive flow from trade events (market orders)
- Compute absorption capacity from depth snapshots
- Compute liquidity fragility from cancellation/replenishment dynamics
- Compute flow/depth ratios as conditional state variables

### Phase 2: Economic Gate (`app/exp012_economic_gate.py`)
- Calculate expected execution cost per event
- Calculate expected gross move from conditional features
- Gate: only proceed when expected_net = expected_gross - cost > 0

### Phase 3: Conditional Model (`app/exp012_model.py`)
- Train conditional state classifier: P(high_impact_state | features)
- Train direction model within high-impact state only
- Train magnitude model for expected move in high-impact state

### Phase 4: Validation (`app/exp012_validation.py`)
- Purged chronological split (train/val/OOS)
- Bootstrap 95% CI on net expectancy
- Walk-forward across sessions
- Robustness: different market regimes (see volatility clustering)

### Phase 5: Replication
- Independent re-run with different random seeds
- Feature ablation (which features drive the conditional state)
